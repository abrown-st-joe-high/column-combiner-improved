Column Combiner

Click and drag to select; arrows (Ctrl+arrow jumps), Tab and Enter move; type, double-click or F2 to edit (arrows then move the text cursor and Enter commits); Delete clears; Ctrl+C / X / V work, including pasting from Excel, Sheets or CSV. Drag the small square at a selection's corner to fill; double-click it to fill down as far as the column to the left has data. Drag a column header onto another to swap columns. Ctrl+D fills down. Formulas start with = (e.g. =B1&"s", =UPPER(A2), =IF(A1="yo","o",C1), =JOIN(", ",A1:A5)); functions: CONCAT, JOIN, UPPER, LOWER, PROPER, TRIM, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, LEN, SUBSTITUTE, REPT, IF, IFERROR, SUM. Tick scratch on a column to use it for building without showing it in practice.
